# PRIME-DRE GCW 2026 
We are happy to announce the 2026 **PRIMatE Data and Resource Exchange (PRIME-DRE) Global Collaboration Workshop (GCW)**. This year’s workshop will again take place online on December 2-3, 2026 (9am-12.30pm EST / 3pm-6.30pm CET / 9pm-12.30am UTC+7). We hope to have an in-person meeting again in the future, but currently lack the funds. 

The GCW will again bring together researchers and data scientists from across the PRIME-DRE network to share insights, explore new methodologies, and co-develop opportunities for collaboration. Whether you're working on neuroimaging, behavioral neuroscience, electrophysiology or metadata, we look forward to hearing your voice in the conversation.
