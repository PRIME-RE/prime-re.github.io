# PRIME-DRE presents a Nature Portfolio Special Collection

After the success of our 2021 [NeuroImage Special Issue](https://www.sciencedirect.com/special-issue/10BKLBFXR17) the PRIME-DRE consortium have now teamed up with Springer Nature to promote a [Nature Portfolio Collection](https://www.nature.com/nature/collections?type=collection) centered on the exciting work people in our consortium drive forward every day.

Nature Collections are collections of articles under a common theme that can be published in participating journals across the Nature portfolio. For this theme, potential journals could be *Nature Communications, Nature Neuroscience, Nature Methods, Communications Biology, Scientific Reports, etc*.

There has been an collection round of *expressions of interest* already, but the collection remains open for contributions that missed out on this initial round. To participate in the collection, submit your work to any of the Nature Portfolio Journals and mention clearly in your cover letter that you intend your submission as part of the **Primate Neuroscience Data Resource and Exchange Collection**. There is no official website, this will go up once manuscripts start getting accepted into the collection.

If you submit, we would appreciate it if you informed the [PRIME-DRE steering committee](steeringcommittee.md) so we can keep track of the collection in progress.

We look forward to seeing your contributions!
